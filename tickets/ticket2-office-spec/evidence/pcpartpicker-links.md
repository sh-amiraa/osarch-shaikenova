# PCPartPicker lists

PCPartPicker does not show prices in tenge and does not sell in Kazakhstan. I used it **only** to check that the parts fit together (socket, memory type, M.2, case size) and to get the power estimate. All real prices are in [../budget.csv](../budget.csv).

Monitors, OS, keyboard/mouse, UPS, headset and webcam are not in these lists (they do not affect compatibility).

Notes:
- PCPartPicker is a US site, so some parts sold in Kazakhstan may be missing. If a part was missing, I used the closest part of the same type and size (written in the list's notes). The real part is in budget.csv.
- Ryzen 8000G CPUs need a 2024+ BIOS on B650 boards. I will ask the shop to confirm the BIOS version before buying.
